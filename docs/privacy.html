Privacy policy

Alam TV Hub is a private app used by one family on its own TV. It isn't offered to anyone else.

What it uses

With the family's permission, the app uses Google's Smart Device Management API to show live video from the family's own Google Nest doorbell and camera, and to know when the doorbell rings.

What it keeps

The Google sign-in details are kept on the family's own home computer. Live video goes straight to the family's TV and isn't recorded or stored.

Who it's shared with

No one. Nothing from Google accounts or Nest devices is sold, shared or used for advertising.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from the third-party connections page of the Google Account.

Contact

Questions can be raised through the app's GitHub page.
